# Trading Strategies

# Purpose

Strategies coordinate market execution behavior.

Strategies are isolated operational systems.

Strategies must remain:
- observable
- auditable
- configurable
- risk-bounded

---

# Current Strategy Categories

## Grid Trading
Range-based execution systems.

---

## DCA
Treasury accumulation systems.

---

## Scalping
Short-duration execution systems.

---

## Arbitrage
Cross-market opportunity systems.

---

## Market Making
Liquidity provisioning systems.

---

## Hedging
Risk mitigation systems.

---

## Volatility Systems
ATR and volatility-aware execution.

---

# Strategy Requirements

Every strategy must:
- expose telemetry
- expose accounting
- expose drawdown visibility
- expose allocation state
- expose execution state

---

# Strategy Constraints

Strategies must never:
- bypass risk systems
- bypass treasury controls
- bypass governance restrictions

---

# AI-assisted Strategies

ACS systems may assist:
- signal interpretation
- volatility analysis
- parameter optimization
- execution recommendations

AI systems must remain bounded.
