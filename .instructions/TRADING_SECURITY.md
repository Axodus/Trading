# Axodus Trading Security

# Philosophy

Security is not a secondary layer.

Security is part of execution itself.

---

# Security Objectives

Protect:
- treasury capital
- DAO assets
- execution integrity
- governance authority
- federation stability

---

# Risk Domains

## Market Risk

Protection against:
- volatility spikes
- liquidation cascades
- illiquid markets
- slippage explosions

---

## Execution Risk

Protection against:
- failed execution
- duplicated execution
- stale orders
- routing failures

---

## Governance Risk

Protection against:
- unconstitutional execution
- unauthorized treasury actions
- governance desynchronization

---

## AI Risk

Protection against:
- rogue agents
- hallucinated execution
- invalid signals
- recursive operational loops

---
