## Calculation and disposition

1. **Least valid entry.** Freeze order type and the venue filter model with the snapshot. With exact decimals, let `G` be the intersection of the venue’s applicable enabled quantity grids with its min/max bounds. Use the grid origins prescribed by the venue, not display precision. Compute `q* = min { q in G : all venue filters for the proposed entry pass }`. For a linear contract at a known positive venue filter price `P_filter` and base-unit multiplier `m`, the notional lower bound on quantity is `N_min / (m × P_filter)`. Round **up** to the first legal grid point at or above that and all applicable quantity minima, then recheck prices, maxima, the leverage bracket and account-specific restrictions. Compare using exact cross multiplication where needed. For dynamic filters use the venue-defined reference and freshness rule; if a conservative valid bound cannot be established, block. Use a separately documented venue model for inverse, quanto or other contracts. Do not submit to discover constraints and do not re-quantize silently after approval.
2. **Exposure, margin and cash needed.** Record venue-filter notional and stressed execution notional separately; for the linear case `N = q* × m × P`. Use the venue’s model for effective initial and maintenance requirements, including bracket, rounding, fee holds and available collateral. `N / 50` is a rough initial-margin illustration for simple linear Futures, not the venue cash requirement. Compare required incremental collateral plus incremental fee, funding and exit reserves against both usable venue collateral and the approved capital budget. Reconcile existing reservations and venue fee holds to avoid counting them twice. Do not count fee rebates, favorable funding or future proceeds toward feasibility.
3. **Loss and liquidation.** Value adverse entry and exit fills for the actual direction, add both fees and applicable adverse funding, and include slippage once (either in price scenarios or as an explicit cost). Separately stress gap, unavailable exit and liquidation costs, including potential account-level effects, to review against the owner’s loss envelope. Use the venue’s margin model or documented calculator for a pre-entry liquidation estimate; do not infer it from `1/50`. Record `distance = (mark − liquidation) / mark` for a long, or `(liquidation − mark) / mark` for a short where applicable; require a positive distance exceeding the owner-approved buffer under the agreed stress. If later authorized and opened, compare the estimate to the venue’s observed position state. A stop trigger is distinct from its fill; neither the stop nor isolated margin is a guaranteed loss cap.
4. **Exit feasibility.** Verify a venue-supported non-increasing exit for the full quantity and every possible partial-fill residual, or a venue-confirmed entry type with provably no partial residual. Check filters, position mode, trigger reference, timeout, how stops and planned exits are coordinated, and who can act if the order is ambiguous. If residual size cannot be closed within the mandate, block; no top-up or second entry to remove dust.
5. **Decision rule.** Record `BLOCKED` for unknown evidence, unsupported 50x, unverifiable isolation, stale settings, no valid minimum, no bounded exit, insufficient collateral, excess stressed notional/loss or inadequate liquidation buffer. Record sizing and failure reason without changing the envelope. A passing worksheet would mean `FEASIBLE_FOR_CTO_REVIEW`, with `REAL_EXECUTION_HOLD` still in force.
