## Proposed composition and contracts

**One entry boundary:** trusted operator → DEV mandate verification → current venue/account reconciliation → atomic risk reservation and durable intent → separately reviewed adapter → per-order and account reconciliation → ledger and evidence. Trading owns the verification and admission boundary. The principal owns the capital decision; the venue is the external source of actual positions, orders, fills, fees, funding and balances. Governance/Treasury, product entitlement and agent capabilities confer no DEV authority. A separate supervisor can stop new risk, then cancel or reduce only within the signed exit scope; expiry/revocation never silently grants a new fallback action.

| Proposed interface | Required content | Admission / persistence invariant |
|---|---|---|
| `DevHumanAuthorizationV1` | `authorizationId`, `experimentId`, authenticated `humanPrincipalRef` and verifiable approval proof, isolated `capitalAccountRef`, personal capital class, venue/product/contract refs, collateral denomination and cap, side, position mode, exact entry quantity and leverage `50`, isolated margin, probe implementation revision/digest, policy digest, validity/revocation, bounded exit and emergency scope | Verify proof, intended account/scope and digest before admitting. Bind to one unique entry intent and its pre-scoped exits. A repeat of the same intent must reconcile/replay; a second or changed entry rejects. Check effective settings before submission. |
| `DEV_CAPITAL_LEDGER_V1` | Account and experiment IDs, currency/scale, entry IDs/sequence, account snapshots, reserved/committed/released margin, signed fills, fees, funding, realized/unrealized PnL, source identities and compensating entries | Store decimals in declared units; reserve and consume in one durable transaction before order I/O. Unknown order outcome retains reservation and blocks new risk; reconcile under restart before resuming. Keep journal/evidence distinct from institutional accounts. |
| `DEV_EXECUTION_EVIDENCE_V1` | Experiment/authorization IDs and digest, runtime/config/policy revision, venue/account and market snapshot refs, request and attempt IDs, client/venue order IDs, acknowledgements, fills and amendments, risk decisions, cancel/exit events, ledger revisions and venue state before/after | Append with source timestamps and stable identifiers; no credentials. Distinguish request, submission, acknowledgement, fills, order reconciliation and account reconciliation. No accepted closure until all are terminal and account/equity reconcile. |

Integration shape for P1/P2 **after separate authorization**: dedicated account and credential binding, allowlisted provider/egress with no arbitrary URL override, dedicated wiring unavailable to R01–R10, and separate DEV storage scope. Keep recovery based on durable attempt/client identity and reject mismatched tenant/principal/resource. Start halted; reconcile positions, regular and conditional orders, balances and prior ambiguous attempts before admitting. Keep account-level halt and exposure reservations durable and concurrent-safe. Emergency exit must be provably non-increasing and bounded to the venue-confirmed exposure; cancellation and fill races must not reverse the position. Halt and escalate if recovery needs an action outside the mandate. No runtime withdrawals, transfers, margin top-ups or automatic exposure increase.
